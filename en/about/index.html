About

We taste first.

Haksan is a coffee house that imports green coffee, roasts Turkish coffee over a wood fire and roasts private label coffee for brands. Our founder, Cevdet Hacıcaferoğlu, is a CQI-certified Q Grader.

Q Grader

We taste the coffee first. Then we sell it.
Cevdet HacıcaferoğluFounder · Q Grader

A Q Grader is a cupper certified by the Coffee Quality Institute (CQI) to evaluate coffee to international standards. Cevdet Hacıcaferoğlu grades the beans and scores them at the cupping table. At Haksan, bean selection, roast approval and private label profiles all pass through this table.

We’re there at every stage of a journey that begins on the farm and ends in the cup.

  1. 01Private label roastingCoffee for your own brand. We define the profile together, roast it and pack it.
  2. 02Haksan coffeesOur wood-fire roasted Turkish coffee, plus espresso and filter coffees, each with its own profile.
  3. 03Green coffeeWe import green coffee and wholesale it to roasters and businesses.
  4. 04Business supplyPlanned coffee supply for cafés, hotels, offices and wholesalers.
